## Issues and community leads

- [DDGS #478](https://github.com/deedy5/ddgs/issues/478) reports rate-limit exceptions collapsing into generic errors. Our HTTP/error/cooldown tests retain distinct classifications. This is an issue author's observation, not a new DDGS test run.
- [OpenSERP #36](https://github.com/karust/openserp/issues/36) reports a CAPTCHA on its first residential-network Google request. It does not establish a population failure rate. CAPTCHA-solving integrations in issues #1/#9 were excluded, not tried.
- [Mwmbl #410](https://github.com/mwmbl/mwmbl/issues/410) discusses upstream result-display/sub-syndication restrictions and unmetered v1 versus v2. This is project discussion, not our access authorization.
- Searches for blocking in sweetcornna/free-search-mcp yielded no matching issues in this bounded search; absence of reports is not reliability evidence.
- Chinese searches included 免费联网搜索 开源, 无需 API Key 搜索, 多搜索引擎 聚合 and 本地 AI 搜索引擎. [360 SkillHub](https://skillhub.360.com/skills/cn-web-search) advertises browser-page retrieval, not an access grant. [telly6/searchpin](https://github.com/telly6/searchpin), [daidaiJ/websearch-mcpserver](https://github.com/daidaiJ/websearch-mcpserver) and [JustSearch](https://linux.do/t/topic/1617070) were additional discovery leads; their deployment/marketing claims were not used as provider authorization. We did not claim a full source audit of these extra leads.
- Reddit searches surfaced [LocalLLaMA's search-provider discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/LocalLLaMA/comments/1v3uyop/what_do_people_use_for_search/) and [Brave CAPTCHA reports](https://www.reddit.com/r/brave/comments/1x8926l/brave_web_search_brave_search_suggestions_are_the/). These are anecdotal leads; only provider documentation/terms and our own native results determine decisions.
